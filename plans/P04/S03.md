## Objective

Replace the current-or-warmest Best-time heuristic with a deterministic remaining-current-day
selection while preserving the entity, options, selected `HH:MM` state, and automation-facing
attributes.

## Changes made

- Added one cohesive pure `best_time.py` domain boundary containing the result, persisted limits,
  complete candidate, three-outcome classification, and frozen lexicographic selection. There is
  no legacy/new algorithm split; the coordinator owns one typed `BestCondition` result.
- Candidates come only from the existing configured-interval forecast samples whose aware Home
  Assistant local time is not in the past and has the current full local date.
- Required finite inputs are symbol, air and feels-like temperature, humidity, sustained wind,
  preceding-hour precipitation, PoP, and thunderstorm probability. Probability endpoints 0/100
  remain valid; missing, malformed, non-finite, or out-of-range inputs make that hour incomplete.
- Applied the persisted temperature range to feels-like temperature and retained inclusive
  humidity, wind, precipitation, and accepted-symbol gates.
- Selected the minimum `(thunder, thermal midpoint distance, PoP, amount, timestamp)` tuple. No
  weighted score or duplicate humidity/wind scoring exists.
- Removed current weather/current time as fallback and clear the entire typed result on every
  calculation.
- Added the available translated `no_suitable_time` state for an exhausted or completely rejected
  healthy current day. Disabled/failed/empty/timestamp-unusable/entirely incomplete data remains
  unavailable. Both states clear dynamic attributes and later complete data recovers in place.
- Preserved selected `HH:MM`, location/time/air-temperature/humidity/precipitation/wind attributes,
  and added apparent temperature, PoP, and thunder probability.
- Updated English/Finnish option wording to identify feels-like limits. The options flow rejects
  every submitted inverted min/max pair without partial persistence. Stored inverted values load
  unchanged for explicit correction and yield `no_suitable_time` meanwhile.
- Updated current time, sensor, and availability maintenance contracts.
